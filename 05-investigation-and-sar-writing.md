# # Merchant Transaction Laundering Investigation

## Financial Crime Investigation & SAR Narrative Practice

> **Note:** This is a fictional training case created for financial crime learning purposes. It does not involve any real customer, merchant, financial institution, or Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filing. The purpose is to demonstrate investigative thinking, risk-based analysis, documentation, and SAR narrative writing practice.

# 1. Case Objective

This case demonstrates the complete investigative process an AML analyst may follow after a transaction monitoring alert is generated.

The objective is to review merchant activity, compare expected and observed behavior, identify potential financial crime indicators, document investigative findings, make a risk-based recommendation, and prepare a SAR narrative using only the available facts.

The scenario focuses on **merchant transaction laundering**, where a merchant account may be used to process payments on behalf of an undisclosed or unrelated business.

---

# 2. Alert Generated

A transaction monitoring alert was generated after unusual changes in merchant processing activity were detected.

**Alert Type:** Unusual Merchant Transaction Activity

**Merchant Type:** Online Clothing & Accessories Retailer

**Risk Rating at Onboarding:** Medium

**Alert Trigger**

* Significant increase in processing volume
* Transaction amounts inconsistent with historical activity
* Elevated refund and chargeback activity
* Generic or unrelated transaction descriptions

---

# 3. Merchant Profile

The merchant was onboarded as an online clothing and accessories retailer.

Expected business activity included:

* Domestic card-not-present transactions
* Average ticket size between **$40 and $150**
* Monthly processing volume of approximately **$25,000**
* Low refund activity
* Low chargeback activity
* Sales related to clothing and accessories

No high-risk business activity or third-party payment processing was disclosed during onboarding.

---

# 4. Observed Transaction Activity

During a 30-day review period, the merchant's processing activity changed significantly.

| Indicator                  |      Expected Activity |             Observed Activity |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------: | ----------------------------: |
| Monthly Processing Volume  |  Approximately $25,000 |        Approximately $185,000 |
| Average Transaction Amount |                $40–150 |                    $650–1,200 |
| Chargeback Activity        |                    Low |       Increased significantly |
| Refund Activity            |                Minimal |    Multiple same-week refunds |
| Transaction Descriptions   | Clothing & Accessories |          Generic or unrelated |
| Customer Geography         |     Primarily Domestic | Multiple foreign-issued cards |

---

# 5. Initial Investigation

The analyst reviewed the merchant profile, onboarding information, transaction history, processing trends, refund activity, chargeback activity, and transaction descriptions.

The observed activity did not appear consistent with the merchant's stated business model. The significant increase in processing volume, higher transaction amounts, elevated disputes, and generic transaction descriptions suggested possible undisclosed business activity requiring additional review.

---

# 6. Key Red Flags Identified

The following indicators were identified during the investigation:

* Significant increase in transaction volume inconsistent with historical activity
* Average transaction amounts substantially higher than expected
* Transaction descriptions not clearly aligned with clothing or accessories sales
* Elevated chargeback activity
* Increased refund activity
* Multiple foreign-issued payment cards
* Activity potentially consistent with processing payments for an undisclosed third party

Taken together, these indicators appeared potentially consistent with **transaction laundering** or **merchant factoring**.

---

# 7. Additional Investigation Steps

Before reaching a final conclusion, the analyst would consider:

* Reviewing the merchant website and advertised products
* Comparing transaction descriptions with stated business activity
* Reviewing chargeback reason codes
* Reviewing customer complaints
* Reviewing previous alerts involving the merchant
* Conducting adverse media and negative news research
* Determining whether transactions suggest undisclosed third-party payment processing
* Consulting Fraud Operations or the BSA/AML team if appropriate

---

# 8. Analyst Assessment

Based on the available information, the merchant's processing activity appears inconsistent with its expected business profile.

The increase in processing volume, larger transaction amounts, generic transaction descriptions, elevated refund activity, increased chargebacks, and foreign-issued payment cards collectively increase the merchant's financial crime risk.

Although the available information does not establish that illicit activity occurred, the observed activity may indicate that the merchant account is being used to process payments for an undisclosed or unrelated business.

The activity is potentially consistent with transaction laundering or merchant factoring and warrants additional review.

---

# 9. Risk Assessment & Recommended Action

**Recommended Action**

Escalate the activity for further AML/Fraud investigation and consider SAR filing if no reasonable explanation is obtained.

**Rationale**

The merchant's observed processing activity differs materially from its expected business model and contains multiple indicators associated with possible merchant transaction laundering.

Additional due diligence is appropriate before determining whether regulatory reporting obligations apply.

# 10. SAR Narrative Practice

During the review period, Merchant A, an online clothing and accessories retailer, processed approximately **$185,000** in card-not-present transactions, significantly above its expected monthly processing volume of approximately **$25,000**. The merchant's average transaction amount also increased from the expected range of **$40–150** to approximately **$650–1,200**.

The activity appeared inconsistent with the merchant's stated business purpose. Several transaction descriptions were generic or did not clearly relate to clothing or accessories sales. The account also demonstrated increased refund activity and elevated chargebacks during the review period. In addition, multiple transactions involved foreign-issued payment cards, which differed from the merchant's expected primarily domestic customer base.

Based on the available information, the observed activity may indicate that the merchant is processing payments for an undisclosed or unrelated business. The activity is potentially consistent with transaction laundering or merchant factoring, where a merchant account is used to process payments on behalf of another entity not disclosed during onboarding.

Given the unexplained increase in transaction volume, larger transaction amounts, elevated disputes, and inconsistency with the merchant's expected profile, the activity was escalated for further AML review.

# 11. AI-Assisted SAR Narrative Prompt

The following prompt was used to support SAR narrative writing practice.

### Prompt

Write a SAR narrative using only the facts provided below.

Do not introduce assumptions, legal conclusions, or information not included in the case.

Use clear, concise, professional language and organize the narrative around:

* Who
* What
* When
* Where
* Why the activity appears suspicious
* How the activity occurred

### Facts

* Merchant A operates as an online clothing and accessories retailer.
* Expected monthly processing volume: approximately $25,000.
* Observed processing volume: approximately $185,000.
* Expected average transaction amount: $40–150.
* Observed average transaction amount: $650–1,200.
* Several transaction descriptions were generic or unrelated to clothing or accessories.
* Refund activity increased.
* Chargeback activity increased.
* Multiple transactions involved foreign-issued payment cards.
* Activity appears inconsistent with the merchant's stated business model.
* Potential typology: transaction laundering or merchant factoring.

### Prompt Boundaries

* Do not include customer names, institution names, or account numbers.
* Do not introduce facts that are not provided.
* Do not state that a crime occurred.
* Do not speculate regarding intent.
* Use objective language such as **"appears inconsistent," "may indicate,"** and **"potentially consistent with."**

# 12. Learning Reflection

This case allowed me to practice the complete investigative process, from reviewing a transaction monitoring alert to documenting findings and preparing a clear, fact-based SAR narrative.

What stood out to me is that effective financial crime investigations involve much more than identifying unusual transactions. Analysts need to understand the expected customer or merchant profile, compare expected activity with observed behavior, evaluate multiple risk indicators together, and document findings using objective, defensible reasoning.

This exercise also reinforced the importance of writing SAR narratives that remain factual, concise, and supported only by the available information. Avoiding unsupported assumptions while clearly explaining why activity appears suspicious is an important part of effective AML investigations.

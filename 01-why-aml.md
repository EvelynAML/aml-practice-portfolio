# Why I Pursue AML

With 14 years of experience in banking and financial operations, I have developed strong skills in transaction review, data accuracy, and compliance.

I am pursuing a career in Anti-Money Laundering (AML) because it allows me to apply my analytical thinking and risk awareness to detect suspicious activities and protect the financial system.

As I transition into the U.S. financial industry, I am actively building AML/BSA knowledge through structured learning, case analysis, and SAR-style writing practice.

This portfolio demonstrates my commitment to developing practical AML skills, including red flag identification, transaction analysis, and regulatory awareness.

My long-term goal is to become an AML/BSA Analyst and contribute to financial crime prevention in the U.S. banking system.

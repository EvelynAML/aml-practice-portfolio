# AML News Analysis

## Case 1: Money Laundering via Shell Companies

**Summary:**  
A group used multiple shell companies to move funds across accounts and hide the origin of money.

**Why It Happened:**
- Weak KYC procedures  
- Lack of ownership transparency  
- Poor monitoring systems  

**Red Flags:**
- Complex ownership structures  
- Unusual transaction patterns  
- High transaction volume with no clear business purpose  

**Prevention Measures:**
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)  
- Beneficial ownership verification  
- Continuous transaction monitoring  

**My Insight:**  
This case shows how important it is to understand both customer identity and transaction behavior in AML investigations.

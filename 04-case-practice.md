# AML Case Practice

## Case 1: Structuring Activity

**Scenario:**  
A customer deposits $9,800 cash multiple times per week.

**Analysis:**
- Repeated deposits just below the $10,000 threshold  
- Pattern suggests intentional structuring  
- Activity inconsistent with normal customer behavior  

**Conclusion:**  
This activity may indicate structuring to avoid reporting requirements under the Bank Secrecy Act.

---

## Case 2: Dormant Account Activity

**Scenario:**  
An account inactive for one year suddenly receives a $50,000 transfer.

**Analysis:**
- Sudden activity after long dormancy  
- Large transaction amount  
- Unusual compared to past behavior  

**Conclusion:**  
This activity may require further investigation due to unusual account behavior.

### Source

### Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the financial crime landscape. Financial institutions increasingly use AI to identify suspicious activity, detect fraud patterns, and strengthen transaction monitoring capabilities. At the same time, criminals are leveraging AI to create more sophisticated schemes, including synthetic identities, deepfake impersonation, and automated fraud attacks. This case explores both the opportunities and challenges AI presents for AML professionals.

### Financial Crime Risks Mentioned

| Risk Type | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Synthetic Identity Fraud | Creation of fake identities using real and fabricated information |
| Deepfake Fraud | AI-generated voice or video impersonation |
| Account Takeover | Unauthorized access to customer accounts |
| Transaction Fraud | Suspicious movement of funds designed to avoid detection |
| Money Laundering | Concealing illicit proceeds through financial systems |

### AML Analyst Perspective

If I were reviewing these activities as an AML Analyst, I would focus on identifying:

- Unusual transaction frequency
- Sudden changes in customer behavior
- Rapid movement of funds
- Multiple accounts connected to one individual
- Transactions inconsistent with customer profile
- Activity involving high-risk jurisdictions

The goal would be to determine whether the activity represents normal customer behavior or potentially suspicious activity requiring escalation.

### How AI Can Help AML Teams

| Traditional Approach | AI-Assisted Approach |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| Manual review | Automated anomaly detection |
| Rule-based alerts | Behavioral pattern analysis |
| Periodic monitoring | Real-time monitoring |
| Basic customer screening | Dynamic risk scoring |
| Spreadsheet tracking | Predictive analytics |

### Limitations and Risks of AI

While AI offers significant benefits, it also introduces challenges:

- False positive alerts
- Data quality issues
- Model bias
- Limited explainability
- Regulatory concerns
- Overreliance on automation
- Potential misuse by criminals

Financial institutions must balance technology with human oversight and risk-based judgment.

### Key Takeaways

- AI is improving fraud detection efficiency.
- Criminals are increasingly using AI-enabled techniques.
- Human judgment remains critical in AML investigations.
- Financial institutions must balance automation with oversight.
- AML professionals need both analytical and technology awareness skills.

### Personal Reflection

This case helped me understand how AI is changing financial crime detection and compliance operations. With over 10 years of banking operations experience involving transaction review, exception handling, documentation controls, and fraud indicator awareness, I recognize that technology can improve efficiency but cannot completely replace human judgment.

My key takeaway is that future AML professionals will need both investigative thinking and an understanding of emerging technologies. AI should be viewed as a tool that supports analysts rather than replacing their decision-making responsibilities.

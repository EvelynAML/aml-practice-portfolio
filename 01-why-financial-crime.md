# Why financial crime

With 14 years of experience in banking and financial operations, I have developed strong skills in transaction review, data accuracy, and compliance.

I am pursuing a career in financial crime because it allows me to apply my analytical thinking and risk awareness to identify suspicious activity, support investigations, and help protect the integrity of the financial system.

As I transition into the U.S. financial industry, I am actively building knowledge in AML/BSA, transaction monitoring, sanctions, and KYC/CDD through structured learning, case studies, and SAR-style writing practice.

This portfolio demonstrates my commitment to developing practical financial crime skills, including transaction monitoring, red flag identification, risk assessment, sanctions awareness, and regulatory compliance.

My long-term goal is to become an AML/BSA Analyst and contribute to financial crime prevention in the U.S. banking system.

# AML Learning Log

## Entry 1: Structuring Awareness

**Date:** April 2026  

**Topic:** Structuring and Reporting Thresholds  

**What I Learned:**  
Structuring is a method used to avoid reporting thresholds by breaking transactions into smaller amounts. Analysts should focus on patterns rather than individual transactions.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Repeated transactions below $10,000 may indicate suspicious activity  
- Patterns are more important than individual transactions  
- Consistency in behavior is critical  

**My Reflection:**  
This helped me understand that suspicious activity is not always obvious and requires pattern recognition and analytical thinking.

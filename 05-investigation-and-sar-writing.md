# SAR Practice

## SAR 1: Structuring Activity

**Scenario:**  
Multiple cash deposits below $10,000 within a short period.

**SAR Narrative:**  
The customer conducted multiple cash deposits below the $10,000 reporting threshold over a short period. This pattern appears structured and may indicate an attempt to evade regulatory reporting requirements. The activity is inconsistent with expected customer behavior and may warrant further review under AML regulations.

---

## SAR 2: Dormant Account Reactivation

**Scenario:**  
Inactive account receives large transaction.

**SAR Narrative:**  
The account, previously inactive for an extended period, received a significant transaction. This sudden change in activity is inconsistent with historical behavior and may indicate potential risk. Further investigation is recommended.
